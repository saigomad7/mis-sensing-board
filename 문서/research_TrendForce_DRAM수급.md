[샘플 데이터] TrendForce Research Note

Executive Summary
Global DRAM bit supply is expected to grow 21% YoY in 2027, revised down from 28%.
The revision reflects slower-than-expected HBM4 yield ramp across major suppliers.
HBM is forecast to account for 32% of total DRAM revenue in 2027.

1. Bit Supply Growth
| 항목 | 2025 | 2026 | 2027(F) |
|---|---|---|---|
| DRAM bit supply YoY | 18% | 24% | 21% |
| HBM bit supply YoY | 65% | 58% | 44% |
| HBM revenue share | 19% | 26% | 32% |

2. Yield Assumptions
HBM4 initial yield is assumed at a level materially below HBM3E at the same stage.
Hybrid bonding adoption is the primary variable. We assume 2~3 quarters of ramp.

3. Pricing
Conventional DRAM contract price: flat in 3Q26, -3~5% in 4Q26.
HBM pricing remains decoupled from conventional DRAM.

4. Risk to View
If hybrid bonding yield improves faster than assumed, 2027 supply could exceed
our base case by 4~6 percentage points, pressuring conventional DRAM pricing.
